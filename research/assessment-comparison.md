## How to read a conflict

When two sources disagree, prefer them in this order:

1. The email or assessment screen for **your** role, level, and country.
2. A page on the company's own domain that names the stage.
3. A platform guide, labelled as the platform's behaviour.
4. A named news article that quotes a spokesperson or an internal document.
5. A candidate post.

A later source does not automatically cancel an earlier one if they describe different stages. Amazon in January 2026 and Amazon in the February 2025 press account are different stages. Atlassian's handbook and Atlassian's principal PDF are different levels.
