# Source register

Research date: **24 November 2026**.

Every claim in the research documents uses one of these labels:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `[Official]` | The company's own career page, interview guide, engineering blog, news post, or candidate instructions. |
| `[Official-Platform]` | Documentation from an interview platform (HackerRank, CoderPad). This describes the product. It does not prove how a given company configures it. |
| `[Reported-Press]` | A named news outlet. Often based on an internal document the outlet says it reviewed. |
| `[Reported-Secondary]` | A prep site or aggregator that quotes a company page. Used only when the primary page could not be retrieved in this pass. |
| `[Reported-Candidate]` | A first-hand candidate account, or an aggregator of such accounts. Never treated as company policy. |
| `[Analysis]` | A conclusion drawn in this project. |
| `[Unverified]` | Searched for on 24 November 2026 and not confirmed. |

Scope notes matter. A Principal Engineer guide is not a new-grad policy. A US pilot is not an India policy. A product a company sells to other employers is not that company's own interview.

## What this pass did not verify

- LinkedIn has not published, on the pages above, a description of an "AI coding round", its duration, its tool, or a rule that prompts are scored.
- Amazon's university OA page and the January 2026 news post describe different assessments. Neither page says which one a new grad in India receives in November 2026.
- Amazon's full interview-loop page (number of rounds, Bar Raiser) was not re-fetched. Do not treat third-party loop diagrams as confirmed here.
- No official Atlassian graduate or India guide containing the AI-Enabled Code Design round was found. The round is in the Principal Backend PDF.
- Google has no careers page in this set that states the code-comprehension pilot. India coverage is unverified.
- Microsoft's live interview-tips page does not contain the "no outside assistance" sentence quoted by some 2026 policy trackers. That sentence is unverified against the page retrieved on 24 November 2026.
- Uber's role-specific interview guide text was not opened. The AI sentence is second-hand.
- Shopify has no published candidate AI-interview rule in the sources found.
- No source here says Cursor, Claude, or Copilot on your own laptop is allowed at LinkedIn, Amazon, or Atlassian.
