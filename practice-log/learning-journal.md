# Learning journal

One entry per session. Copy the blank template. Keep the failures. They are the curriculum.

Scores use the rubric in [mock assessments](../interview-preparation/mock-assessments.md). Leave the scores blank on an ordinary practice day and fill the notes anyway.

## How to write an entry

- Say what you asked the model to do, in one sentence. Paste the prompt if it was short.
- Say what was wrong with the answer, or "I checked two claims and both matched the file."
- Say the command you ran and the result.
- Name one change for the next session. Make it a drill, not a mood.
